The Manual

HOW WE PLAY

You don't have to have a plan. Walk in cold, we'll get you rolling — first paint set, first deck, first squad.

LEVEL 01

BROWSE

Come in and dig. New arrivals every week — Warhammer boxes, singles, sealed cards, paints and hobby tools. Ask anyone on the floor; they've painted this stuff.

LEVEL 02

PAINT

Build your palette. Every major paint line stocked and open for questions. Bring the mini, get advice, leave with the right primer, wash, and metallic.

LEVEL 03

PLAY

Find a table. Weekly events, launches and prereleases at both stores. Show up with a deck, an army, or a curious kid — we'll find you a game.
