The House Rules

Browse. Paint. Play.

The whole loop under two roofs — pick out the model, find the paint, find the game. No appointment, no hard sell, no snobs.

I.

Browse

New arrivals every week — Warhammer boxes, singles, sealed cards, and the shelves you can lose an afternoon in. Ask anyone on the floor; they've painted this stuff.

II.

Paint

Every major paint line — Vallejo, Scale 75, Two Thin Coats, Monument, Citadel, Tamiya. Bring the mini, get the color match, leave with the right primer.

III.

Play

Weekly events, launches and prereleases at both stores. Show up with a deck, an army, or a curious kid — we'll find you a table and a game.

Walk-ins welcome during shop hours · Questions? support@dragonslairhobbies.com

Before You Come In

Good Questions.

Quick answers for painters, players, collectors, and first-time visitors.

Do I have to be a Warhammer or Magic player to come in?

No. The doors are open — beginners, kids, curious partners, all welcome. The staff have painted every model you'll see, and half of them started at zero. Come dig around, ask questions.

Can I bring a mini or a deck and get help?

Absolutely. Bring the model to color-match paints, or bring a deck to spar or get a tune. Painting hangs are on the calendar, and the tables sit open for pickup games most nights.

How do I know if there's an event tonight?

The event calendars live on the store pages (Wallingford & Berlin — links in Visit). For live updates and last-minute changes, the Discord is the fastest signal.

Which store should I go to?

Whichever's closer. Wallingford (220 North Colony St) is open Sunday–Saturday. Berlin (848 Farmington Ave) is closed Mondays. Hours & live status are in Visit below.

Do you have singles / do you buy singles?

Yes on both — Magic, Pokémon, Lorcana and more. Bring the binder in, we'll take a look.

Special orders / hard-to-find kits?

If Games Workshop or one of our paint distributors still stocks it, we can usually get it in. Email support@dragonslairhobbies.com or ask at the counter.
